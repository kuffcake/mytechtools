DAY BY DAY

The full itinerary

Fourteen days, compressed from a longer wish list. Notes at the bottom cover what got trimmed and how to add it back if you extend the trip.

01In flight
Los Angeles → Berlin
Flight, overnight13–15 hrs

Depart LAX in the evening. This is a lost day to time zones — plan to sleep on the plane.

02Berlin
Arrival day
Germany

Arrive midday, rest and adjust. An easy first walk: Brandenburg Gate, Unter den Linden, Pariser Platz.

OVERNIGHT — BERLIN
03Berlin
Museum Island & the Reichstag
Germany

Full day: Museum Island (Pergamon / Neues Museum), the Reichstag Dome — book ahead — and Checkpoint Charlie.

OVERNIGHT — BERLIN
04Day trip
Berlin → Potsdam → Berlin
GermanyTrain, 45 min each way

Morning train to Potsdam for Sanssouci Palace and its park, plus Cecilienhof. Back to Berlin by evening for the East Side Gallery or Berlin Wall Memorial if time allows.

OVERNIGHT — BERLIN
05Move day
Berlin → Wittenberg → Leipzig
GermanyTrain, 1h45m + 39m

Morning free in Berlin, then train to Wittenberg — the Castle Church where Luther posted the 95 Theses, about two hours is enough. Continue to Leipzig, arriving evening.

OVERNIGHT — LEIPZIG
06Move day
Leipzig → Dresden
GermanyTrain, 1h15m

Leipzig morning: St. Thomas Church (Bach's church) and the Monument to the Battle of the Nations. Afternoon train to Dresden — the Zwinger Palace and a lit-up Frauenkirche after dark.

OVERNIGHT — DRESDEN
07Border day
Dresden → Wroclaw → Krakow
Germany → PolandTrain, 3h40m + 3h

Dresden morning at leisure (Brühl's Terrace). Train to Wroclaw — use the layover to walk the colorful Rynek if the gap is 2+ hours. Continue to Krakow, arriving evening. The longest travel day of the trip.

OVERNIGHT — KRAKOW
08Krakow
Old town & Kazimierz
Poland

Wawel Castle and Cathedral, the Old Town Rynek, St. Mary's Basilica, and the Kazimierz (former Jewish) district.

OVERNIGHT — KRAKOW
09Krakow
Auschwitz-Birkenau
Poland~1.5 hrs each way

Full-day trip to the memorial and museum. Book the guided tour slot weeks ahead in high season. An emotionally heavy day — keep the evening low-key.

OVERNIGHT — KRAKOW
10Border day
Krakow → Vienna
Poland → AustriaTrain, ~5h40m

Travel day. The line passes close enough to Bratislava to add it as a stop here, or save it as a half-day trip from Vienna instead — either way it doesn't need its own dedicated day.

OVERNIGHT — VIENNA
11Vienna
Palaces & the Ringstrasse
Austria

Schönbrunn Palace, St. Stephen's Cathedral, the Hofburg, and a walk around the Ringstrasse.

OVERNIGHT — VIENNA
12Move day
Vienna → Munich
Austria → GermanyTrain, 4h30m

Late-morning train to Munich. The route passes near the Mauthausen Memorial (near Linz) — only worth the detour with real schedule flexibility, since it adds a half-day.

OVERNIGHT — MUNICH
13Munich
Marienplatz & one big choice
Germany

Marienplatz and the Glockenspiel, Nymphenburg Palace. Then choose one: the Dachau Memorial (half day, sobering) or a day trip to Neuschwanstein Castle — doing both needs a 15th day.

OVERNIGHT — MUNICH
14Flight home
Munich → Nuremberg → Los Angeles
Germany → USATrain 1h30m + Flight 13–15 hrs

Morning train to Nuremberg for the Castle and the Nazi Documentation Center. Back to Munich (or fly direct from Nuremberg if your airline serves it) for an evening flight home. Crossing time zones, you land in LA the same day.

A note on fitting this into 14 days: the original wish list — 4–5 days Berlin, 3–4 Vienna, 2 each for Munich and Nuremberg, plus Bayreuth — adds up closer to 16–18 days. This version trims Berlin to 3.5 days, Vienna to 2, Munich to 1.5, and drops the second Nuremberg day and Bayreuth entirely. To restore them, either add 3–4 days to the trip, or cut one leg (e.g. skip Wroclaw/Krakow/Auschwitz and go straight from Dresden to Vienna).

Also worth flagging: the original notes list "Krakow → Venice," but the travel time given (5h40m) matches Krakow → Vienna, not Venice — this itinerary assumes Vienna is correct.
ALONG THE WAY

Landmarks & day trips

What's actually in the plan, and what's close enough to add if the trip grows a few more days.

Berlin & Potsdam GERMANY

Brandenburg Gate & Reichstag Dome In city

Free to visit; book the Reichstag dome slot online in advance.

Museum Island In city

Pergamon and Neues Museum — the Pergamon's main hall is partly closed for renovation, check ahead.

East Side Gallery In city

The longest surviving stretch of the Berlin Wall, now open-air murals.

Sanssouci Palace 45 min by train

Frederick the Great's summer palace in Potsdam — already on the itinerary.

Wittenberg, Leipzig & Dresden GERMANY

Castle Church, Wittenberg In town

Where Luther posted the 95 Theses; the old town is UNESCO-listed.

St. Thomas Church, Leipzig In city

Bach was cantor here for 27 years — the Bach Museum sits next door.

Zwinger Palace & Frauenkirche In city, Dresden

The Frauenkirche is stunning lit up after dark.

Bastei Bridge, Saxon Switzerland 45–60 min from Dresden

Dramatic sandstone cliffs.

NEEDS AN EXTRA HALF DAY

Wroclaw & Krakow POLAND

Rynek, Wroclaw In city (layover)

The colorful market square — worth it only if your layover is 2+ hours.

Wawel Castle & Cathedral In city, Krakow

Seat of Polish kings for centuries.

Auschwitz-Birkenau ~1.5 hrs each way

Book the guided tour slot weeks ahead in high season.

Wieliczka Salt Mine 30–45 min from Krakow

UNESCO site; an underground chapel carved entirely from salt.

OPTIONAL ADD-ON

Vienna, and the border crossings around it AUSTRIA

Schönbrunn Palace In city

Book skip-the-line tickets in advance.

St. Stephen's Cathedral & Hofburg In city

Climb the south tower for the best rooftop view of Vienna.

Bratislava Old Town ~1 hr from Vienna

The easiest extra country on the whole trip — Slovakia, almost on the direct rail line.

OPTIONAL — EASY ADD
Mauthausen Memorial Near Linz, on the Vienna–Munich line

Former concentration camp, similar tone to Dachau and Auschwitz.

OPTIONAL ADD-ON

Munich & Nuremberg GERMANY

Marienplatz & Glockenspiel In city

Shows run at 11am and noon, plus 5pm from March–October.

Nymphenburg Palace In city (tram)

Summer residence of the Bavarian royals.

Dachau Memorial or Neuschwanstein Castle 25 min / ~2 hrs from Munich

Pick one for Day 13 — very different days, both worth it.

Salzburg Old Town ~1.5 hrs from Munich

The easiest cross-border day trip from Munich, if a 15th day opens up.

GREAT EXTENSION
Nuremberg Castle & Documentation Center In city, Nuremberg

Panoramic old-town views, and an excellent museum on the rise of the Nazi regime.

Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Bamberg, Bayreuth 1–2 hrs from Nuremberg

All trimmed from this 14-day version for time.
